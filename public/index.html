Underwriting Methodology (Demo)

1. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR): Evaluates total monthly EMI burdens against net income. (Note: The 40%–60% demo benchmarks used here are configurable illustrative limits; actual bank limits vary).

2. Tier Gating: Prime credit scores (≥ 750) qualify for maximum borrowing leverage. Scores below 650 indicate conditional underwriting or security requirements.

3. Deterministic Separation: All financial scores and sanction limits are produced by deterministic mathematical algorithms. AI coaching provides educational literacy only.
