How Eligibility Is Calculated

1. Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR): Measures the percentage of your monthly income committed to paying debt. Traditional banks mandate that total debt installments remain under 40%–50% of income.

2. Credit Tier Gating: Applicants with credit scores ≥ 750 receive standard prime eligibility. Scores below 650 are flagged for conditional requirements or co-applicants.

3. Mathematical Certainty: Approval determinations are calculated 100% by deterministic code algorithms. AI is strictly reserved for qualitative financial coaching.
